    How the engine decides

    2 · Weighted sum or geometric mean

    By default each cell scores S = Σ wᵢ·fᵢ with weights normalised to one. With constraints on, cells above +2 km, steeper than 15° or beyond ±50° score zero regardless of weights. Optional No compensation switches to a weighted geometric mean so a near-zero criterion cannot be bought back by others.

    3 · The globe

    A sphere displaced by real MOLA elevation (−8.2 km in Hellas to +21 km on Olympus Mons), lit per pixel from the same raster, and textured with the Viking Orbiter colour mosaic against a procedural galactic starfield.

    4 · Route planner

    Multi-hazard A* over an 8-connected, longitude-wrapping grid. Each step costs distance × (1 + k · (slope + rough_pen + dust_pen)), penalising steep grades, rough sub-cell relief (>50 m) and low-TI fine dust traps (<100 tiu); slopes above the limit are impassable and candidate rest stops (flat, smooth, firm terrain; no shelter implied) are spaced ≥200 km apart.

    5 · Dust-storm power & thermal model

    Daily solar energy is 3.0 kWh/m²/sol × S(φ, Lₛ) × e−0.35 (τ − 0.5) with ~0.28%/sol clear-sky dust settling (3× in storm), fitted so τ ≈ 10.8 reproduces Opportunity's 2018 loss. Habitat heating charges only the deficit above electrical waste heat, and interior temperature tracks any heating shortfall.

    6 · Season, radiation & weight sensitivity

    Seasonal insolation S(φ, Lₛ) uses Mars's 25.19° obliquity and orbital eccentricity (e = 0.0934, perihelion at Lₛ = 251°). Atmospheric column mass follows from MOLA elevation (16.4 g/cm² at datum, 11.1 km scale height) for chronic GCR dose (anchored to Curiosity RAD: 0.64 mSv/day at −4.4 km) and a unified exponential attenuation law (λ = 15 g/cm²) for a design-basis August-1972-class SPE assumption. The Monte Carlo test perturbs every weight 30 times to measure Top-5 ranking stability.

    7 · What this model does not do

    • Resolution. About 5 km per pixel (TES layers ≈ 10 km): meter-scale boulders and small craters are not resolved; regional terrain class reflects MOLA relief only, and landing ellipses require HiRISE imagery (linked per site).
    • Elevation steps. The global MOLA layer is quantised at ≈ 115 m, so slopes are regional gradients.
    • Ice coverage. SWIM maps ±60° latitude consistency only (datasets agree vs. concentration/yield); outside ±60°, ice is unknown and scored zero.
    • Not modelled. Local topographic horizon shadowing, active array-cleaning cycles, and propellant boil-off.

    8 · Validation benchmark & terrestrial portability

    • Orbital benchmark (9 landers + 39 HLS-2015 zones). Against 200 random-longitude draws per ±5° latitude band, the 9 historical Mars landers rank at the 90th percentile (p < 0.005, weighted sum; 89th geometric mean) and all 39 NASA HLS-2015 Exploration Zones rank at the 71st percentile (p < 0.005, both sum and geometric mean).
    • Terrestrial DEM portability. The same constraint-veto MCDA kernel, antimeridian-aware A* slope router, and solar-battery attenuation engine work cleanly on terrestrial elevation rasters for emergency base siting, search-and-rescue routing, and off-grid microgrid sizing; GeoJSON exports load directly into QGIS and Leaflet.
