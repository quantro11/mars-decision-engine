    Method

    How the engine decides

    1 · Suitability per criterion, 0 to 1

    CriterionDatasetRuleWhy it matters

    2 · Weighted sum or geometric mean

    By default each cell scores S = Σ wᵢ·fᵢ with weights normalised to one. With constraints on, cells above +2 km, steeper than the model's 15° planning boundary or beyond ±50° score zero regardless of weights. Optional No compensation switches to a weighted geometric mean so a near-zero criterion cannot be bought back by others.

    3 · The globe

    A sphere displaced by real MOLA elevation (−8.2 km in Hellas to +21 km on Olympus Mons), lit per pixel from the same raster, and textured with the Viking Orbiter colour mosaic against a procedural galactic starfield.

    4 · Route planner & Mode Separation

    Multi-hazard A* over an 8-connected, longitude-wrapping grid. Each step costs distance × (1 + k · (slope + rough_pen + dust_pen)), penalising steep grades, rough sub-cell relief (>50 m) and low-TI fine dust traps (<100 tiu). Pressurized Rover mode (~20 km/sol) places terrain rest stops ≥200 km apart; Foot EVA / Marswalk mode (2.4 km/h nominal) disables 200 km rover stops and models 1.5 km micro-rests, CO₂ RCA scrubber saturation, and a protected 25% return reserve. EVA uses the Lunar xEMU reference architecture extrapolated to Mars 0.38g / 6–10 mbar CO₂ (not flight-certified Mars hardware).

    5 · Dust-storm power & weather models

    Daily solar energy is 3.0 kWh/m²/sol × S(φ, Lₛ) × e−0.35 (τ − 0.5) calibrated so τ ≈ 10.8 reproduces Opportunity's 2018 loss. REMS/MEDA rows are typical values for each rover site (not a single archived sol); InSight data come from its archive. Both can be extrapolated to another site by elevation (hydrostatic) and latitude. NASA DONKI alerts model 1.524 AU propagation & transit delay separately from instantaneous surface radiation.

    6 · Season, radiation & Monte Carlo weight sensitivity

    Seasonal insolation S(φ, Lₛ) uses Mars's 25.19° obliquity and orbital eccentricity (e = 0.0934, perihelion at Lₛ = 251°). Atmospheric column mass follows from MOLA elevation (16.4 g/cm² at datum, 11.1 km scale height) for chronic GCR dose (Gale Crater Curiosity RAD 0.64 mSv/day ± 18.8% uncertainty band) and a design-basis August-1972-class SPE assumption (λ = 15 g/cm²). The Monte Carlo test perturbs every weight 100 times and reports 95% Wilson binomial confidence intervals on Top-5 stability.

    7 · Scale and data traceability

    • Scale. Global layers (~5 km/px MOLA, ~10 km/px TES, SWIM 2.0) cannot resolve sub-hectometer boulders or craters. Walking routes need HiRISE stereo terrain models (1.0–2.0 m/post, <3% of Mars). The app does not model terrain finer than its 100 m Tactical EVA cells.
    • Tactical EVA (16 km²). For 9 sites the app ships 4 km × 4 km boxes at 100 m/cell cut from the MOLA-HRSC 200 m DEM and THEMIS 100 m day-IR mosaic. Hazards smaller than ~100 m (boulders, small craters) remain invisible; certifying a traverse needs HiRISE stereo terrain models.
    • Caves & Ice Boundaries. MGC³ cave entries are unconfirmed orbital thermal/optical anomalies (Cushing et al. 2015), not verified shelter tunnels. SWIM maps ±60° consistency only; outside ±60° is labeled NO_DATA (distinct from ice-free).

    8 · Validation

    • Orbital benchmark (9 landers + 39 HLS-2015 zones). Against 200 random-longitude draws per ±5° latitude band, historical Mars landers rank at the 90th percentile (p < 0.005, weighted sum; 89th geometric mean) and all 39 NASA HLS-2015 Exploration Zones rank at the 71st percentile (p < 0.005).
    • Suit energy model vs Apollo (Test 3). At lunar gravity the suit metabolic model gives ~271 W, inside the Apollo 14–17 per-EVA range (≈228–352 W, mean 272 W; NASA TN D-7883). Its one free constant is calibrated to that mean, so this is a consistency check; Mars values are extrapolations.
    • Dust-devil risk index. A hand-tuned heuristic on physical features with modelled (not live) inputs. Shown as a 0–100 index; no accuracy is claimed.

    9 · Tactical EVA 16 km² Blend, Power Domain, Field Assimilation & Provenance

    • Local Blend & Field Propagation Formula. Each 4 km × 4 km (40×40, 100 m/cell) box blends center conditions and local orbital data via V_cell = (1 − w) · V_center + w · V_local (w = 0.50 default in Advanced). Manual in-situ measurements in Section D override the target cell and propagate radially across neighboring cells using compact-support smoothing k(d) = (1 − (d / R)²)² (default R = 300 m) with confidence boost and orbital discrepancy warning when difference exceeds 50%.
    • 8 Scored Domains (incl. Power & Energy). Scores integrate Thermal, Radiation, Topography, Optics, Geology, Elevation & Slope, Dust, and Power & Energy using the active STORM solar/MMRTG + trace-heater budget. Daily energy deficit sets Power = 0 and caps Final Compatibility at ≤ 20 (VETO).
    • Orbital Data Sources & Integrity. Pre-cut bundles (4 preset sites + 5 balanced candidates) integrate USGS/MOLA-HRSC 200 m Blended DEM slope (Mars_HRSC_MOLA_BlendDEM_Global_200mp_v2) and 100 m THEMIS Day IR relative brightness (Mars_MO_THEMIS-IR-Day_mosaic_global_100m_v12) as normalized thermal/optical modulation (labeled MODEL, never fake TI).
    • Vehicle Config & Constraints. All vehicle specs live in web/vehicles.json and web/tactical_config.json with explicit source and verified flags; placeholder/fallback sources render as ESTIMATE / TAHMİN.

    10 · Autonomous Flight Architecture & Robotics Kernel (Quantro Q-11)

    • ROS 2 & Embedded Daemon Integration. Direct dual-mode execution via robotics/mars_decision_ros2_node.py running natively under ROS 2 (Humble/Iron/Rolling) or as a zero-dependency headless Linux daemon on onboard rover/flight avionics (Jetson Orin, Raspberry Pi).
    • MAVLink WPL 110 Waypoint Protocol. Export MAVLink (.waypoints) writes the Top-5 candidate coordinates in the QGroundControl / ArduPilot / PX4 waypoint format, for integration tests with standard autopilot tooling (Earth autopilots do not navigate on Mars).
    • Hardware-in-the-Loop (HIL) test bridge. robotics/hil_telemetry_bridge.py streams JSON telemetry packets over TCP/WebSocket; by default it generates simulated IMU, barometer and battery values so the console can be tested before real hardware is attached.
    • Systems Architecture. Designed and engineered by Quantro Flight Systems.
