    02 // Weighted Synthesis & Hard Vetoes

    Composite score is evaluated per pixel as S = Σ wᵢ·fᵢ with weights normalized such that Σ wᵢ = 1. When NASA HLS-2015 hard constraints are active, any cell violating elev > +2000 m, slope > 15°, or |lat| > 50° is strictly vetoed (S = 0).

    03 // 3D MOLA Planetary Globe Engine

    The 3D view displaces a 240×120 spherical mesh radially using real MGS MOLA DEM elevations (−8.2 km Hellas floor to +21.2 km Olympus Mons summit) and computes per-pixel normal perturbation from the 2048×1024 elevation raster.

    04 // Slope-Constrained A* Traverse Planner

    Operates on an 8-connected spherical-metric grid with longitudinal wrapping at ±180°. Edge transition cost is d(a,b) × (1 + k·slope_b), with cells exceeding maxSlope treated as impassable barriers.

    05 // Global Dust-Storm Power Balance Model

    Daily horizontal solar flux is modeled as E = 3.0 kWh/m²/sol × cos(φ) × exp(−0.35(τ − 0.5)), calibrated to reproduce the MER Opportunity June 2018 global storm attenuation (τ ≈ 10.8 → ~97% array loss, 650 to 22 Wh/sol).

    06 // Stated Engineering Limitations

    • Spatial Resolution: Grid resolution is ≈10.3 km/px at the equator; meter-scale hazards (boulders, small secondary craters < 5 km) require HiRISE local reconnaissance.
    • Vertical Quantization: Global MOLA DEM layer is quantized at ≈115 m steps; derived slopes represent regional kilometric gradients rather than meter-baseline lander footpad slopes.
    • SWIM 2.0 Coverage: Subsurface ice consistency maps span ±60° latitude; higher latitudes are scored 0 for ice and excluded by the ±50° NASA HLS thermal/power constraint.
    • Unmodeled Environmental Factors: Galactic cosmic ray (GCR) shielding, seasonal Ls orbital eccentricity insolation variance, and electrostatic dust-devil clearing are omitted.
